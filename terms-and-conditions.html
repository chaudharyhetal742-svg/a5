3. Reservation Policies, Communal Seating Protocols, and Punctuality

Our dining salon operates on an intimate communal model with two twenty-foot shared oak tables seating twenty-four guests per evening service. Because courses are choreographed and served simultaneously to all dinner guests in an unhurried sequence, punctuality is essential; guests are requested to arrive at our Mercer Street salon fifteen minutes prior to the scheduled 7:00 PM commencement of evening service to ensure a relaxed transition into the dining room.

We understand that unexpected travel delays occur in metropolitan environments; however, guests arriving more than thirty minutes late may miss early hearth courses, which cannot be recreated without disrupting the collective timing of the communal banquet. Tables are assigned thoughtfully by our hospitality team to foster engaging table dialogue, and seats cannot be rearranged on the evening of service without prior coordination with our concierge staff.

4. Dietary Accommodations, Allergies, and Patron Responsibilities

SupperBanquet takes food allergies and dietary sensitivities with utmost seriousness, preparing thoughtful parallel dishes when given advance notice. Patrons are strictly required to notify our dining concierge of any severe food allergies, celiac disease, or specific ingredient intolerances at least seventy-two hours prior to their reserved feasting date to allow our kitchen brigade to procure certified allergen-free ingredients from our farm partners.

While our kitchen maintains stringent sanitization protocols and dedicated prep stations for sensitive meals, our hearth and bakery handle wheat, tree nuts, dairy, and shellfish daily in an open culinary layout. Therefore, while we exercise extreme vigilance, SupperBanquet cannot guarantee an absolute zero-particulate environment for hyper-sensitive airborne allergies, and patrons dine with full awareness of our open artisanal kitchen environment.

5. Cancellation Policies, Rescheduling Windows, and Deposit Terms

Due to the limited seating capacity of twenty-four guests per evening and the perishable nature of our fresh farm-to-table ingredients, reservation deposits are non-refundable. Cancellations made at least seven days prior to your reserved feasting date may be transferred to a future open date within six months of the original booking, subject to seasonal seat availability in our dining salon calendar.

Cancellations submitted within seven days of the scheduled dinner, or failure to attend without notice, result in the complete forfeiture of the reservation deposit to offset kitchen procurement and staffing commitments. Patrons are fully permitted to transfer their reserved seats to friends, family members, or colleagues, provided our concierge desk is notified of the guest name change at least twenty-four hours prior to evening service.
