Data Storage Protocols

Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective immediately across supperbanquet.com

1. Fundamental Principles of Web Cookies and Digital Telemetry

Cookies are minute alphanumeric data packets and text files placed onto the storage drive of your desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone device when you navigate digital websites across the internet. These files act as a decentralized technical memory for web servers, enabling the website to recognize your unique browser session, preserve navigation preferences, maintain security barriers, and provide aggregated analytical telemetry regarding site functionality.

SupperBanquet believes that cookie utilization should be transparent, strictly bounded, and completely respectful of personal patron autonomy. We deploy digital cookies solely to guarantee smooth technical navigation, protect our reservation request forms against automated spam attacks, and measure high-level website traffic metrics. We never utilize invasive cross-site tracking cookies that follow your browsing activities across unrelated commercial portals, nor do we monetize cookie profiles with commercial advertising brokers.

2. Detailed Categories of Cookies Utilized on supperbanquet.com

Our digital platform utilizes a limited suite of cookies classified into three transparent operational tiers: strictly necessary operational cookies, user preference cookies, and aggregated performance measurement cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are mandatory for the core architecture of our website to function properly; they maintain active reservation form fields, manage CSRF security verification tokens, and preserve your mobile navigation drawer states as you browse through our seasonal feasting editions.

User preference cookies enable our website to remember choices you make during your visit—such as whether you have previously viewed our seasonal tasting menu or dismissed our newsletter subscription banner. These functional files ensure you do not experience repetitive interactive prompts and provide a refined, seamless user experience tailored to your personal aesthetic preferences across consecutive browsing sessions on our web domain.

3. Analytical Telemetry and Google Analytics Implementation

To evaluate website responsiveness, monitor visitor traffic volumes, and identify technical bottlenecks in page load times, SupperBanquet utilizes Google Analytics via the standardized global site tag (gtag.js) property identifier G-0LY0HY7L01. This analytical tool places first-party cookies that collect anonymous, aggregated telemetry regarding how visitors discover and navigate through our collections, including session duration, bounce rates, and browser display resolutions.

Importantly, our Google Analytics implementation operates with strict IP address anonymization enabled by default, ensuring that individual visitor IP addresses are truncated before processing and storage. We strictly prohibit the transmission of personally identifiable information—such as full names, private email addresses, or specific residential shipping coordinates—into our analytical telemetry reports, utilizing this data exclusively to optimize website rendering speeds and improve page navigation.

4. Comprehensive Patron Control, Cookie Disabling, and Browser Management

You maintain absolute, sovereign control over how cookies are stored on your personal devices and have the legal right to inspect, restrict, block, or delete cookies at any time through your web browser configuration preferences. All modern internet browsers—including Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge—provide intuitive preference settings that allow users to reject third-party cookies, clear existing cache files, or mandate notification whenever a server attempts to deposit a cookie.

Should you choose to configure your browser to block all cookies universally, you will still be fully capable of reading our Gastronomy Journal articles, viewing our dining salon photography, and reviewing all tasting menus across supperbanquet.com. However, please note that disabling essential session cookies may occasionally impact interactive capabilities, such as automated reservation form validation or responsive mobile drawer navigation during active browsing sessions.

5. Periodic Regulatory Revisions and Ongoing Contact Coordinates

As international data protection regulations evolve and digital web technologies advance, SupperBanquet may occasionally modernize and revise this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our technical architecture, statutory legal standards, or analytical measurement protocols. Any formal revisions will be updated directly upon this page with an amended effective date, and continued navigation of our domain following the publication of changes signifies your ongoing acknowledgement of the modernized policy terms.

If you have questions regarding our use of cookies, desire additional clarification regarding our analytical implementations, or wish to submit feedback regarding our digital data protocols, please reach out directly to our technological administration team at tech@supperbanquet.com. You may also contact our dining concierge desk in writing at SupperBanquet, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by calling our customer service representatives at +1-888-777-5845.
