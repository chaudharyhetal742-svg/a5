Legal & Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective immediately across supperbanquet.com

1. Comprehensive Privacy Overview and Patron Commitment

SupperBanquet ("we," "our," or "the Salon") operates the digital portal at supperbanquet.com to showcase our communal evening dining experiences, wood-fired hearth menus, and culinary philosophy. We are firmly committed to upholding the highest standards of digital privacy, personal data security, and confidentiality for every guest, patron, and website visitor who interacts with our Mercer Street dining salon. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact mechanisms through which personal details are collected, processed, safeguarded, and eventually archived or deleted across our web services.

When you browse our online seasonal feasting menus, submit reservation requests for our communal tables, or subscribe to our seasonal guest register, we believe you have an absolute right to understand how your information is handled. We do not engage in commercial data harvesting, predatory tracking routines, or third-party behavioral profiling. Every piece of information gathered is collected solely to provide an elevated hospitality experience, manage seating arrangements, and ensure our culinary brigade accommodates your dietary preferences with precision.

2. Categories of Information Collected by Our Systems

Our web infrastructure collects information through two transparent pathways: voluntarily provided personal correspondence and automated technical request telemetry. Voluntarily provided data includes your full name, electronic mail coordinates, telephone contact number, party size, and specified dietary requirements submitted when requesting banquet seats or contacting our dining concierge. Without these verified details, our hospitality team cannot confirm reservation deposits or prepare tailored parallel courses for guests with unique food sensitivities.

Automated technical request information is standard across modern web architecture and is captured instantaneously by our secure server logs whenever a browser requests an asset from our domain. This telemetry includes your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system build, browser user-agent specification, referring universal resource locator, display viewport dimensions, and granular request timestamps. This non-personally identifiable telemetry allows our engineering team to diagnose server latency, defend against automated bot exploitation, and optimize page load speeds for high-resolution culinary photography across mobile and desktop devices.

3. Lawful Basis and Concrete Purposes for Data Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international statutory frameworks, SupperBanquet processes personal data exclusively under legitimate, lawful business justifications. Primarily, processing is mandatory for the execution of contractual and pre-contractual dining relationships, such as confirming communal table reservations, coordinating private salon buyouts, generating deposit receipts, and communicating essential arrival protocols. We never process personal details without an explicit, verifiable operational requirement.

Additionally, we process contact information under your freely given, informed consent when you opt into our email communications regarding newly released seasonal feasting dates, harvest reports from our Hudson Valley farm partners, and private culinary salon invitations. You maintain the autonomous right to revoke this consent instantaneously at any time by selecting the dedicated one-click unsubscribe mechanism integrated into every digital dispatch we transmit, or by emailing our dining concierge directly with your withdrawal request.

4. Data Storage, Cryptographic Security, and Retention Limits

All sensitive electronic communications, reservation request transmissions, and database records are safeguarded utilizing modern cryptographic standards, including Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols and AES-256 bit data-at-rest encryption algorithms. Our internal network infrastructure is shielded by redundant web application firewalls, intrusion detection monitors, and strict multi-factor authentication requirements enforced across all personnel with authorized access to guest communication portals. Physical reservation logbooks maintained in our New York dining salon are stored securely in locked staff offices.

We adhere to strict data minimization principles and retain customer correspondence only for the minimum duration required to achieve the stated business purpose or comply with federal tax and commercial recordkeeping statutes. General dining inquiries and past reservation notes are retained for a maximum of twenty-four months to assist with repeat booking preferences, after which all digital logs are permanently purged through secure cryptographic overwriting protocols. Financial transactional records required under United States commercial statutes are archived securely for seven years before definitive certified destruction.

5. Non-Disclosure and Third-Party Service Provider Standards

SupperBanquet maintains an unyielding corporate stance against the commercial monetization of patron information: we have never sold, rented, leased, or bartered customer data to third-party advertising brokers, behavioral tracking syndicates, or commercial lead generators. We share data strictly with trusted infrastructural partners who operate under rigorous data processing agreements and are legally prohibited from utilizing your personal information for any independent purpose outside our verified instructions.

These vetted service providers include secure enterprise cloud hosting infrastructure located within certified domestic facilities, encrypted email delivery systems for sending booking confirmations, and secure reservation management software that processes guest details without storing unencrypted financial card numbers. In extraordinary circumstances, we may disclose personal data if formally mandated by valid legal subpoenas, court orders, or statutory regulatory mandates issued by competent law enforcement bodies exercising lawful jurisdiction.

6. Global Privacy Rights, Data Access, and Contact Channels

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, SupperBanquet extends comprehensive privacy prerogatives to all patrons. You possess the legal right to request formal confirmation regarding whether your personal information is stored within our databases, obtain a complete machine-readable copy of your personal data file, demand the correction of inaccurate contact records, or instruct our team to permanently expunge all non-statutory records associated with your identity under the universally recognized "Right to be Forgotten."

To exercise any of these privacy rights, or to submit formal inquiries regarding our data handling methodologies, please submit a written communication to our designated Data Protection Officer at privacy@supperbanquet.com. Alternatively, inquiries may be dispatched via registered postal mail to SupperBanquet Atelier, Attention: Legal & Privacy Affairs, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by telephoning our dining concierge directly at +1-888-777-5845 during standard salon hours.
