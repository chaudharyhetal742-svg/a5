Our Culinary Salon

An Ode to the Convivial Evening Feast

Founded at 181 Mercer Street in New York, SupperBanquet was born from a desire to strip away modern dining pretension and return to the sacred core of gastronomy: fire, grain, and shared fellowship.

Our Hearth Philosophy

We believe that food cooked over live wood embers carries an elemental depth that gas appliances cannot replicate. Our culinary team works exclusively with split white oak and applewood to roast heirloom squash, wild coastal fish, and heritage grains.

Every evening, we open our doors to twenty-four guests. We invite you to slow down, break crusty hearth bread with your neighbors, savor botanical zero-proof infusions, and experience dinner as it was intended: an unhurried communal celebration.
